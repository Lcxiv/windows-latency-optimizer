LatencyGuard — new look, take 1

low-fi wireframes. 4 distinct directions. pick what clicks, we'll build it in hi-fi after.
the roast

what's broken right now

  • visual language = 2014 gamer optimizer. dark navy + cyan + emoji.
  • symptom picker floats in 70% empty void. weak hero.
  • emoji icons on a kernel-level tool. not serious enough.
  • simple/expert toggle = couldn't decide, built both.
  • generic "100 health score" donut. your real data is way more interesting.
  • findings page buries 855ms input gap under "all clear".
  • 4 random accent colors, none mean anything.
  • chrome eats the top 8% for no reason.

4 directions below

  • 01 · Instrument. scientific, monospace-first, numbers are the hero.
  • 02 · Conversation. single question per screen, cinematic.
  • 03 · Timeline. before / after / now, stopwatch metaphor.
  • 04 · Command deck. one dense screen, pro tool vibe.

03 · Timeline
before · after · proof
concept
A stopwatch for your system.

Every scan is a snapshot on a timeline. Apply a fix → new snapshot → visual A/B. The whole product is framed around improvement over time. Solves your "history" tab problem by making history the main axis.
